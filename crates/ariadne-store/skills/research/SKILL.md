# Research

A finding without a source is an opinion.

## Steps

1. Write the question in one sentence. Agree it before you start.
2. List what an answer needs. Name the sources that can give it.
3. Gather the evidence. Record where each fact came from.
4. Test the leading answer against the evidence that contradicts it.
5. Write the report: question, findings, recommendation, confidence.

## Rules

- Cite the source of every fact: a link, a file and line, or a command and its
  output.
- Separate what you measured from what you inferred.
- Give the strongest case against your own recommendation.
- Say what you did not check.
- Where the evidence does not settle the question, say so. Do not pad it.

## Done

The report answers the question, names its sources, states a recommendation
with its confidence, and lists what would change it.
